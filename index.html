Potential Gemini Use Cases

Three practical frameworks designed to solve immediate student challenges, utilizing AI to facilitate critical thinking rather than replacing it.

Turn lecture slides into an exam plan

File upload & Guided Learning

Upload slide decks and utilize AI to generate sequential quizzes. When gaps in knowledge appear, step-by-step reasoning guides students through complex concepts to identify weak spots proactively.

“Here are my lecture slides for chapters 4 to 6. Quiz me one question at a time, starting easy. When I get one wrong, walk me through the reasoning step by step before moving on.”

Practice interview answers out loud

Custom Gem & Voice Interaction

Build a customized conversational agent to act as an interview coach. This serves as vital preparation for asynchronous video interviews, teaching candidates how to articulate answers clearly without live feedback.

“You're a recruiter for a summer analyst role. Ask me one behavioral question at a time and wait for my answer. Then give feedback using the STAR format: what was clear, what was missing, and a tighter version of my answer.”

Deep Research for Professional Networking

Deep Research Analysis

Generate cited corporate briefs in minutes, allowing students to redirect their preparation time toward writing highly tailored, insightful questions for industry coffee chats and informational interviews.

“Build a one-page brief on [company]: how it makes money, notable news from the past six months, and three thoughtful questions I could ask an analyst there. Cite every source.”

Re-evaluating Academic Support

During my time mentoring incoming business students, a clear pattern emerged: students rarely need direct answers. What they need is a framework to uncover what they don't yet understand, and the support to work through it. This is why adapting structured, guided learning technology is crucial for the transition to higher education.
